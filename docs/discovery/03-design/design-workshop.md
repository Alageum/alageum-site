# Design Workshop — 90–120 минут

## Required participants

2–4 Alageum representatives: **ONE Final Design Decision Owner**, brand/marketing, content owner,
one public/B2B business representative. More reviewers may provide input asynchronously but not
parallel final approvals.

## Pre-work

Material register entries for brandbook, vector logos, fonts/licenses, existing websites,
photo/video/3D and competitor/reference list. Participants independently mark references by
specific useful/forbidden qualities, not «нравится».

## Agenda

1. Confirm public-site audiences vs authenticated B2B audiences.
2. Map top journeys and decisions each screen must support.
3. Confirm brand constraints, mandatory/prohibited patterns, accessibility/locales/devices.
4. Review existing assets and ownership/licensing gaps.
5. Agree information architecture/content responsibility and realistic approval checkpoints.
6. Decide comment consolidation, approval evidence, SLA and change-after-approval process.

## Blocking questions

- `[P0][NEEDS_CLIENT]` Who has final approval authority?
- Who comments, who consolidates, and who can reopen an approved decision?
- Which brandbook/version/legal constraints are authoritative?
- Which journeys/screens are required for first release and acceptance?
- What materials are unavailable and who produces them by when?

## Outputs

Decision owner, audience/journey list, material gaps, references with rationale, design constraints,
approval calendar, Decision Log entries and scope impacts.
