## Usage readiness versus answer readiness

The package is ready to conduct Discovery. Client answers are intentionally not ready: all business, integration, design, production and deployment decisions remain `[NEEDS_CLIENT]` until interviews and evidence are completed.

## Initial P0 blockers to resolve

1. Authorized Project Owner and scope/acceptance decision authority.
2. One Final Design Decision Owner and comment consolidation process.
3. ERP/1C owner, exact system/configuration, integrator and test/interface access feasibility.
4. Master-system ownership for core entities.
5. Business definition of B2B Organization and multi-legal-entity/multi-membership rules.
6. Production infrastructure and information-security owners/topology constraints.
7. UAT and Scope Freeze signatories.

These blockers do not prevent scheduling kickoff; they are the first kickoff outputs.
