# Discovery meeting plan

Встречи не заменяют requested evidence. После каждой обновляются notes, Open Questions, Decision Log, material register и scope impact.

| # | Meeting | Participants | Duration | Required inputs | Key questions | Expected outputs | Blocking prerequisites |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project Owner / Kickoff | Sponsor, Project Owner, PMs, proposed decision owners | 60–90m | Contract/current scope, stakeholder candidates | Goals, authority, constraints, systems, cadence, blockers | Governance, stakeholder map, meeting schedule, initial P0 register | Project Owner available |
| 2 | Design / Brand / Marketing | Final Design Owner, UX/brand/marketing/content, JSJ design | 90m | Brandbook/assets/references/content inventory | Audiences, journeys, approval, prohibited/required patterns | Design brief, material gaps, approval process | Final Design Owner nominated |
| 3 | Catalog / Technical Product | Catalog owner, engineers/product experts, marketing, data owner | 90–120m | 5–10 varied anonymized products, taxonomy/export | Families, variants, attributes, documents, search, master | Draft taxonomy/data templates/source decisions | Product expert and samples |
| 4 | Sales / RFQ / CRM | Sales owner, CRM admin/integrator, support/marketing | 90–120m | Forms, funnel, CRM objects/fields/status samples | Lead/RFQ/deal flow, deduplication, ownership, exceptions | Process map, CRM scope, passport actions | CRM owner/access evidence |
| 5 | ERP / 1C | ERP owner, 1C admin/developer/vendor, business owners, security/network | 120m | Version/config/docs, entity samples, sandbox status | Interfaces, masters, entities, IDs, volume, failures | ERP passport draft, Data Source Matrix decisions, access plan | Correct technical specialist present |
| 6 | Finance / Documents / EDO | Finance/accounting, legal, document owner, EDO specialist | 120m | Anonymized invoices/contracts/acts; provider docs | Visibility, roles, freshness, types, signing, retention | Financial/data and document matrices, EDO passport actions | Finance/legal owners |
| 7 | Production / OTK | Production owner, OTK/quality, ERP/MES specialist, customer service | 90–120m | Real stage export and examples | Actual stages, mapping, dates, visibility, exceptions | Lifecycle/status mapping and sources | Status evidence available |
| 8 | Logistics / Service / Warranty | Logistics, service/warranty, quality, privacy/security | 90–120m | Shipment/service/complaint samples | Tracking visibility, identifiers, warranty, SLA, files | Process maps, provider needs, acceptance cases | Process owners present |
| 9 | IT / Infrastructure / Security | Infra owner, network, DBA, security/SOC, DevOps | 120m | Standards, topology/network diagram, policies | Hosting, environments, connectivity, identity, backup, NFR | Constraints, security matrix, readiness actions | Authorized technical owners |
| 10 | AI / Knowledge Base | AI business owner, knowledge owners, security/legal/privacy, SMEs | 90–120m | Candidate cases/sources and AI policy | Public/B2B, allowed data/tools, provider constraints, acceptance | Approved candidate matrices and prohibited actions | Security and data owners present |
| 11 | UAT / Acceptance | Product Owner, confirmed UAT reps, QA, module owners | 90m | Draft Final ТЗ, journeys, matrices | Evidence, severity, entry/exit, test data, sign-off | UAT plan and Acceptance Matrix owners | Stable draft scope |
| 12 | Final Scope Review | Authorized scope/cost/schedule decision makers, leads | 120m | Consolidated artifacts, open P0/P1, CR assessments | Included/excluded, limits, dependencies, unresolved items | Final ТЗ approval path, Scope Freeze or blockers | All P0 closed or explicitly escalated |

## Suggested order

Meetings 3–4 may run in parallel after kickoff/design ownership. Meetings 5–8 depend on named domain/technical owners. Meeting 9 must precede final integration/security commitments; meetings 11–12 occur only after artifacts are consolidated.
