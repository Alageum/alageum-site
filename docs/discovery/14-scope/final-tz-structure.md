# Final ТЗ structure

Каждый раздел заполняется подтвержденными фактами, решениями и ссылками на evidence. Неизвестное остается `[NEEDS_CLIENT]`, а не заменяется mock-поведением.

1. Общая информация и стороны
2. Цели, business outcomes и success criteria
3. Термины и сокращения
4. Stakeholders, governance и decision process
5. Пользователи, организации и lifecycle
6. Роли, permissions и approval authority
7. Публичный портал и content model
8. UX/UI, brand, accessibility, locales
9. Каталог, taxonomy, product data и search
10. B2B кабинет и cross-module journeys
11. RFQ / commercial request
12. Orders и customer-visible lifecycle
13. Finance
14. Documents, files, retention
15. Production/status mapping
16. Logistics
17. Service, warranty, complaints
18. CRM integration
19. ERP/1C integration
20. EDO/signature integration
21. Notifications/external services
22. AI use cases, tools, safeguards, acceptance
23. Data ownership and Data Source Matrix
24. Migration/initial loading/reconciliation
25. Security/privacy/audit
26. Infrastructure/environments/deployment
27. Non-functional requirements
28. Integration passports and field mappings
29. Testing, UAT and Acceptance Matrix
30. Release, migration, rollback and go-live
31. Training, documentation and handover
32. Scope Limits and quantitative bounds
33. Client responsibilities, dependencies and assumptions
34. Explicit exclusions/future scope
35. Change Request procedure
36. Appendices: diagrams, samples, matrices, decisions

## Final readiness

Final ТЗ cannot be approved until traceability exists from goals → requirements → source/decision → implementation scope → acceptance evidence.
