## Limits

- No dimensions, tolerance, exact SKU match, catalogue rating equivalence, hidden internals or unpictured equipment certified.
- Individual radiator fin counts are illustrative; separate-unit counts and leaf-stack orientation are source-grounded.
- Minor brackets, pipes, terminal carriers, cabinet controls and fasteners are simplified. Extra unlabelled roof insulators are represented by visible shape only; their electrical purpose is not inferred.
- For p115 and p123, unestablished fan internals remain opaque source-visible cooling housings; no total hidden fan count is asserted.
- CPU rasterization only; interactive browser controls, WebGL/GPU shading, camera input and disposal lifecycle are outside this visual approval.
- Icons are compact oblique schematics. Close terminals and source-side groups may overlap visually at small sizes; they are not source-view-accurate construction drawings.
- Approval expires for any changed reviewed library/source/render dependency; subsequent repairs require verification.
