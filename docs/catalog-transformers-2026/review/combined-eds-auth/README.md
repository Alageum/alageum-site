## Observation contract and security boundary

Every default-reader proof invocation captures raw bytes and rereads its entire
consumed closure before returning. Explicit readers retain the established
uncached semantics. Missing/tampered inputs, wrong predecessor pairs, extra or
incomplete closure, report/approval mismatches, mixed successors, pending review,
persistent warm-cache mutation and mid-invocation mutation are rejection cases.
The unchanged contract is sequential observation, not an atomic filesystem
snapshot: a transient change restored between observations or a change after its
final read remains outside the guarantee.

Actual cryptographic signing is unavailable and the flags default off. Synthetic
provider tests do not establish Kazakhstan certificate validity, trust, OCSP,
time stamping, company authority or legal signature validity. Google real-client
credentials, live-provider/browser acceptance, HTTPS/proxy rollout review and
official eGov product/access requirements remain open. Dependency audits and
all release, browser, storage and operational gates remain separate obligations.
No push, deployment, real signing, provider registration or agreement is granted
by this amendment.
