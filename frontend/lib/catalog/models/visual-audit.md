# Construction visual audit

Audited 2026-10-01: all 65 imported families, all 59 available source-image crops, and the text-only source pages. Navigation classification is independent of this mapping.

## Coverage

- 238 official records stay separate
- 123 source-matched records use 20 shared outer-construction forms
- 91 records retain an original source illustration because their construction is special, mixed, or not sufficiently matched
- 14 website-only references remain explicitly generic
- 10 records across 6 imported families have no verified construction image

“Source-matched” means the common construction shown for a family, not an exact CAD model, dimensions, internal circuit or guaranteed appearance of every supplied execution.

## Important exceptions

- КТП 25–250 and КТПНД use cabinet-plus-open-tank geometry; МТП uses a pole
- КТПН and 2КТПН have separate single/double enclosure forms
- Я5000/ЯТП use wall enclosures, not standing cabinets
- БДРМ variants found only in electrical schematics do not inherit the dimensioned enclosure
- УКЗВ overhead-input variants share the insulator form; cable-input variants remain source-only
- УКЗН uses the separately pictured tall floor cabinet, without upper insulators
- БКТП-2500 points to page 38; 2БКТП-2500 points to page 39
- ШНН and mixed ПТМ/ТДЕ families remain source-only, except the explicitly captioned ПТМ(Д)-У1 construction
